Screenshot Intelligence

Can a local classifier organize saved screenshots?

A CS 513-B study of lightweight supervised models and human-assisted labels.

The research sample

1280 images across public sources.

1255 content-labeled screenshots. The negatives are flower photographs. Personal intent needs owner review.

The supervised experiment

Logistic regression, linear SVM, k-NN and Complement Naive Bayes.

Source labels and explicitly marked training pseudo-labels supply targets. RapidOCR ONNXRuntime and frozen CLIP supply features. Fresh-folder classification uses the saved classifiers.

What the scores cover

8 content categories have trained class boundaries.

Some targets are Qwen pseudo-labels. Source-only tests cover their available classes. Agreement with machine labels is reported separately. Source styles limit generalization.

Live demonstration
