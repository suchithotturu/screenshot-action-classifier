# Third-party models, datasets and sample media

Raw research datasets and annotation databases are not redistributed here. Acquisition scripts download from the original sources and preserve source provenance locally. Check upstream terms before reuse or redistribution.

- **CLIP:** OpenAI's pretrained visual representation, [official repository](https://github.com/openai/CLIP), MIT license. The setup script downloads the [Xenova ONNX conversion](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/clip-vit-base-patch32) at pinned revision `6ef1ebc` and verifies SHA-256 `583fd1110a514667812fee7d684952aaf82a99b959760c8d7dca7e0ab9839299`.
- **Qwen3-VL 4B Instruct:** optional local annotation model, [official model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct), Apache 2.0. Distributed separately through [Ollama](https://ollama.com/library/qwen3-vl:4b-instruct). Qwen is absent from the core classification path.
- **Ollama:** optional local runtime, [official repository](https://github.com/ollama/ollama), MIT license. Setup downloads its official macOS release; its package contains separately licensed components.
- **MASC:** [authors' repository](https://github.com/Ali-Aahmed/MASC-Dataset), MIT release. Android UI screenshots provide source mappings and additional Qwen-annotated content examples. Original source metadata is used for grouping/acquisition, never as classifier features.
- **Enrico:** [authors' dataset page](https://userinterfaces.aalto.fi/enrico/) and [repository](https://github.com/luileito/enrico). Reading screenshots were used privately for this study. Screenshot redistribution licensing is recorded as unverified; no Enrico screenshot is committed here.
- **TensorFlow Flowers:** [official tutorial and download reference](https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials/images/classification). The downloaded archive includes per-image photographer attribution and CC BY 2.0 notices; these are retained locally by the preparation script. Flower photographs supply non-screenshot and Miscellaneous examples. No flower image is committed here.
- **CORD:** Park et al./NAVER, [official repository](https://github.com/clovaai/cord) and [official dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/naver-clova-ix/cord-v2), CC BY 4.0. Receipt images supply source-labeled receipt examples; no receipt image is committed here.
- **Demo portrait:** NASA astronaut Eileen Collins photograph from the [scikit-image astronaut sample](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut), public-domain NASA sample. Other demo phone screens are generated by `fixtures.py` and depict fictional content.

The fitted classifier and aggregate results were produced for this course project from those sources. Third-party dependencies retain their respective licenses. Publishing this repository does not change upstream licenses.
