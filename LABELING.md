# Labeling guide

First establish capture provenance. A phone photo, downloaded graphic or standalone meme may contain the same subject as a screenshot. Choose **Unknown** when the available evidence does not establish capture. Choose **False** for an ordinary photo. Content labels apply to photos and screenshots alike. Owner-purpose and usefulness fields remain restricted to the matching owner and screenshot task.

## Choose the dominant content

| Label | Apply it when… |
| --- | --- |
| Messages & conversations | A conversation, DM or email thread is the object being saved. A friend’s deadline message belongs here. |
| Social & entertainment | Commentary, a joke, a reaction or an entertainment post dominates a confirmed screenshot. |
| Places & recommendations | A map, venue, restaurant or location recommendation dominates, including within a social feed. |
| Products & offers | The screen centers on an item, price, offer or subscription. A gym membership offer belongs here. |
| Reading & information | The main object is an article, tutorial, educational post or set of instructions. |
| Receipts & confirmations | The screen records a completed purchase, booking, ticket or payment. |
| App screens | The main object is a generic interface, dashboard or settings screen. |
| Other | The screenshot is understood and falls outside the seven preceding classes. Any source can use this label. |

Use **Unknown** for unresolved labels. Prefer a shoe listing over its social host app, and a conversation over the product mentioned in one of its bubbles. A confirmed concert ticket is proof. A venue-centric listing is a place. An information-heavy event announcement can be reading. Use tags such as education, fitness, food or travel for secondary content.

## Ask why it was saved

A matching owner may select **act on it**, **try or buy later**, **keep proof or reference** or **share or fun**. A receipt often serves as proof, but a plausible use alone cannot establish the owner's reason. Public purpose stays unknown. Only the owner can say whether an image is still useful today. The approval records that time and preserves previous revisions.

Actions may be empty or contain several suggestions. Use a visible quote as evidence and enter a date only when the image supports it. Inspect automatic OCR separately. A corrected transcript remains a human annotation and never enters model features. Flag ambiguity and approve a valid class even when descriptions, transcripts or actions are empty.

## Review conditions

An assisted reviewer can compare source targets, editable values and the original Qwen proposal. An independent blind reviewer opens an unexposed record without those labels and locks their judgment by approving it. Record the genuine reviewer role. A separate owner should never be represented by a reviewer guessing their intent.
